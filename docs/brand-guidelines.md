# Fork presentation and terminology

Use **WebUI Workbench** as the distribution/repository name and **unofficial Open WebUI Desktop fork** as its description. It is not presented as independently authored Open WebUI software or as an official product.

Keep upstream Open WebUI logos/names visible in the embedded application and retain authorship/license notices. Do not imply endorsement or invent a new logo that obscures that provenance.

Write direct, factual English for primary documentation, with a German entry point. Prefer “optional connector”, “selected workspace”, “creates a commit” and “tested compatibility” over vague marketing language.

Never claim “nothing leaves your computer”, “all integrations are ready”, “fully sandboxed”, “official Garmin integration”, “zero maintenance” or “always conflict-free updates”.

Distinguish a discoverable template from an enabled connection, endpoint reachability from a verified tool call, a workflow dispatch from a successful deployment, and a static preview from a running cloud application.

Keep release/download/support links on this repository. Links to upstream must be labeled as upstream. Screenshots and examples must not expose personal connections or data.
